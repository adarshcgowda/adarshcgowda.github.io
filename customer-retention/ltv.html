Limits. The history is only two years, so at early forecast dates the later months of a cohort's life are learned from one or two older cohorts. Forecast dates overlap, so the checks are not independent. Spending is revenue, not profit, undiscounted. The method learns spending by age, so it does not know that winter months sell more. Treat the result as a calibrated range, not a promise.
