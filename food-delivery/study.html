What slows a delivery, and what the Bangalore restaurant market looks like

A one-off study with a point of view: which factors move delivery time, how much a model can explain, whether Bangalore is different, and how to read restaurant ratings honestly.

Read with care. The delivery data behaves more like generated data than raw logs (see Data quality): effects are unusually clean and cities are almost identical. The findings demonstrate the method; they are not market facts.
